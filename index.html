ABOUT THE PROJECT

What is Packet Journey?

When you connect to a website, your request doesn't simply travel directly from your computer to a server. It passes through multiple systems that help move information across the internet.

Packet Journey visualizes that process. Enter a destination and the application resolves its domain, identifies the destination's network information, measures browser HTTP response time, and visualizes a conceptual route a packet could take.

01 DNS

Converts a domain name into an IP address.

02 ROUTING

Networks forward packets toward their destination.

03 RESPONSE

The destination receives the request and responds.

TECHNICAL NOTE

The destination data and HTTP measurement are real. The hop-by-hop route shown above is currently a conceptual visualization. A future backend will provide actual traceroute data.
